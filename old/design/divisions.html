Vertical Integration

Operational Divisions

Our manufacturing facilities are equipped with state-of-the-art technology, ensuring precision from fiber to finished product.

Step 01

Spinning Division

The foundation of our quality lies in our spinning units. We procure high-grade cotton to produce yarn that meets international standards. Our spinning facilities are capable of producing a wide range of counts, catering to the diverse needs of the global denim and knitting industries.

  • High-grade raw cotton sourcing
  • Coarse to fine yarn counts
  • Advanced automated machinery
Step 02

Weaving Division

Our weaving units are equipped with the latest high-speed Air Jet looms. This allows us to manufacture premium quality fabrics with varied widths and weaves. We focus on producing defect-free grey fabric that serves as the perfect base for our finished products.

  • High-speed Air Jet Looms
  • Diverse weave structures (Percale, Satin, Twill)
  • Strict quality control protocols
Step 03

Finishing & Stitching

This division brings our textiles to life. We have a complete processing facility for dyeing, printing, and finishing fabrics. Our stitching unit converts these fabrics into high-end home textile products such as bed linen, curtains, and kitchen textiles, ready for global retail shelves.

  • Advanced dyeing and printing
  • Automated stitching lines
  • Export-ready packaging
Support

Power Generation

To ensure uninterrupted production and meet strict delivery timelines, Zahidjee Textile Mills has established its own power generation plants. We use a mix of gas and diesel generators to maintain self-sufficiency, ensuring operational efficiency 24/7.

  • Self-sufficient energy production
  • Mix of Gas and Diesel Engines
  • Ensures 100% uptime for production
