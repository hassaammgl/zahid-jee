Premium Quality

Our Portfolio

From finest yarns to luxurious home textiles, our products define excellence in the global textile market.

Yarn Products

We produce premium quality yarn ranging from coarse to fine counts. Our spinning units are optimized for producing Carded, Combed, Siro, Lycra, and Slub yarns. These are widely used in the manufacturing of Denim and Knitting products globally.

Product Varieties

100% Cotton Polyester Cotton (PC) Chief Value Cotton (CVC) Slub & Siro Lycra Blends

Fabric Products

Our weaving division creates the canvas for luxury. We produce high-quality Greige and Processed fabrics using state-of-the-art Air Jet Looms. From basic weaves to complex dobbies, our fabrics are known for their durability and flawless finish.

Weave Types

Percale (T-180 to T-400) Satin / Sateen Twill & Drill Duck Fabric Dobby Designs

Finished Products

We are specialists in Home Textiles. Our stitching units transform our premium fabrics into luxurious bedding and linen products. We export to top retailers in the USA, Europe, and UK, ensuring every stitch meets international quality standards.
